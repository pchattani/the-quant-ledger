Pure factors

The risk model's view

A characteristic factor model after toraniko: every day the cross-section of stock returns is regressed on sector dummies (constrained to sum to zero) and standardised style scores, weighted by the square root of market value. The coefficients are the return to one unit of a style with everything else held at zero. The long/short books are then regressed on them: what each book really loads on, and what is left.

Research

Does the score rank returns?

A book's Sharpe says one construction made money. These ask the earlier questions of the score itself, in its sector-neutral form: whether it orders names by the return of the following month, for how long, in which part of the cross-section, at what cost and for how much money, and whether the result is a new one or a known style again. Definitions were fixed from the literature before any of this was run; the window from 2024 is a holdout, shown and never used.
